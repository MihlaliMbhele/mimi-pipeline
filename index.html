📋  Weekly Research Update — MIMI Data Pipeline

2 May 2026

What this diagram shows

This is the full data pipeline for the Municipal Infrastructure Management Index (MIMI) — a composite index I am developing as part of my PhD at UCT to assess how efficiently and effectively South African Water Services Authorities manage infrastructure.

The pipeline starts from source documents stored on SharePoint (Annual Financial Statements, MuSSA feedback reports, AGSA audit outcomes, Blue & Green Drop reports) and ends with a scored, validated index for each municipality per year, disaggregated into two sub-indices: IMEI (Infrastructure Management Efficiency Index) and IMFI (Infrastructure Management Financial Index).

What has been completed this week

  • All source documents uploaded to SharePoint with full metadata tagging
  • SharePoint metadata scripts built and tested (AFS, MuSSA, MFMA Annexure)
  • Duplicate MuSSA files cleaned from local store (8 removed, 650 remain)
  • Full pipeline architecture designed and documented (this diagram)
